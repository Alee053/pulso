# Pulso Context

Pulso helps people discover and evaluate startup opportunities. This glossary records the product terms already visible in the prototype; terms remain provisional until product requirements are finalized.

## Discovery

**Startup**:
A company profile presented for discovery, including its pitch, sector, stage, team, and traction.
_Avoid_: listing, opportunity, company card

**Founder**:
A person identified as part of a startup's founding team.
_Avoid_: owner, user

**Interest**:
A signal selected by a person to describe what they want to discover or support.
_Avoid_: filter, tag, preference

**Discovery feed**:
An ordered set of startup profiles presented for evaluation.
_Avoid_: list, search results

**Recommendation**:
The explanation or signal that describes why a startup is relevant to the selected interests.
_Avoid_: score, ranking

## Person actions

**Saved startup**:
A startup a person keeps for later review without necessarily expressing active interest.
_Avoid_: favorite, bookmark

**Interested startup**:
A startup for which a person has explicitly expressed interest.
_Avoid_: liked startup, match
